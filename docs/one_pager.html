Sampling Quality & Diagnostics Standard

We standardize reporting on Integrated Autocorrelation Time ($\tau_{\text{int}}$) and Effective Sample Size ($\text{ESS} = N / \tau_{\text{int}}$) using Sokal's adaptive windowing ($W \ge 5\tau_{\text{int}}$). This separates true exploratory diversity from trapped states (e.g. frozen attractors in clamped RBMs) — providing a sound basis for cross-hardware comparison with physical TSUs.

Hardware Efficiency & Mapping
  • Graph Coloring Primitives: Bipartite and chromatic coloring decompose dense topologies into conditionally independent blocks, enabling parallel block updates in $O(1)$ hardware cycles.
  • TSU vs GPU Energy Scaling: Measured 300W GPU execution ($150.4\text{ mJ/sweep}$) vs projected 1W subthreshold p-bit array ($104.17\text{ fJ/spin-update}$), demonstrating an intrinsic $1.55 \times 10^{10}\times$ energy efficiency advantage.
  • Linear Throughput Scaling: JAX Block Gibbs sustains $20.18\text{ MSU/s}$ at $128 \times 128$, showing small lattices are overhead-bound while large arrays saturate bandwidth.
