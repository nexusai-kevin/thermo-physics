## MCMC Sampling Diagnostics (`uv run thrml-bench diagnose`)

Method: 500 warmup sweeps, then 4000 samples spaced one full 2-colour block-Gibbs sweep apart. τ_int = 1 + 2·Σ_{k=1..W} ρ(k) with Sokal automatic windowing (W ≥ 5·τ_int), clamped at τ_int ≥ 1; ESS = N / τ_int. τ_int is in sweeps (the integrated autocorrelation, i.e. mixing time of the observable).

At the benchmark setting the clamped RBM is a near-deterministic attractor: free spins that never flip have zero variance, so autocorrelation is undefined for them and they are excluded from the worst-case τ_int. ESS for such a chain says nothing about exploration, which is why a hot setting (β = 0.15) is reported alongside.

## Combinatorial Routing: Pure Sampling vs. Hybrid 2-Opt (Exp 9)

Evaluated across 5 independent restarts on a 100-spin permutation lattice ($10 \times 10$ assignment matrix, 28 chromatic color blocks). The reference optimum is computed exactly using the Held-Karp dynamic programming algorithm ($L_{\text{opt}} = 6.296$).

- **Pure Sampling:** Best tour read directly from sampled states that form exact permutation matrices (no 2-opt, no repair heuristic).
- **Hybrid:** The exact same pure sampled tour followed by classical 2-opt local search until convergence.

## Experiment 10: Sherrington-Kirkpatrick (SK) Frustrated Spin Glass

### Overview
Evaluates thermodynamic Gibbs sampling against the canonical dense spin glass problem with Gaussian quenched disorder $J_{ij} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1/N)$. Unlike 2D planar Ising systems, the SK model features dense $O(N^2)$ non-planar couplings and high frustration, inducing a rugged free-energy landscape with exponentially many metastable states.

### Benchmark Results ($N=24$)
* **Exact Ground-State Energy ($2^{24}$ Brute-Force):** $E_0 = -15.4266$ ($E_0/N = -0.6428$)
* **Best Sampled Energy:** $E_{\text{best}} = -15.4266$ (**100.0% Exact Ground State Match**)
* **Sokal Autocorrelation Time ($\tau_{\text{int}}$):** 14.82 steps
* **Effective Sample Size (ESS):** 438.6 uncorrelated samples / 13,000 recorded iterations
* **Mean Absolute Overlap $\langle |q| \rangle$ (Low $T$):** 0.5215 (cluster condensation in low-lying Parisi valleys)
* **Overlap Variance $\text{Var}(q)$ (Low $T$):** 0.3345 (broad bimodal support indicating multi-valley RSB exploration)

### High-$T$ vs. Low-$T$ Phase Contrast & Classical SA Comparison ($N=24$, Seed 42)

| Method | Temp / Schedule | Best Energy | Rel Gap | $\langle \|q\| \rangle$ | Wall Time |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |

* **Classical SA Baseline:** Converges to the global minimum ($E = -15.4266$) within 0.055s over 30,000 single-spin Metropolis updates with incremental local field updates.
