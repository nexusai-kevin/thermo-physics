## MCMC Sampling Diagnostics (`uv run thrml-bench diagnose`)

Method: 500 warmup sweeps, then 4000 samples spaced one full 2-colour block-Gibbs sweep apart. τ_int = 1 + 2·Σ_{k=1..W} ρ(k) with Sokal automatic windowing (W ≥ 5·τ_int), clamped at τ_int ≥ 1; ESS = N / τ_int. τ_int is in sweeps (the integrated autocorrelation, i.e. mixing time of the observable).

At the benchmark setting the clamped RBM is a near-deterministic attractor: free spins that never flip have zero variance, so autocorrelation is undefined for them and they are excluded from the worst-case τ_int. ## Experiment 10: Sherrington-Kirkpatrick (SK) Frustrated Spin Glass

### Overview
Evaluates thermodynamic Gibbs sampling against the canonical dense spin glass problem with Gaussian quenched disorder $J_{ij} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1/N)$. Unlike 2D planar Ising systems, the SK model features dense $O(N^2)$ non-planar couplings and high frustration, inducing a rugged free-energy landscape with exponentially many metastable states.

### Benchmark Results ($N=24$)
* **Exact Ground-State Energy ($2^{24}$ Brute-Force):** $E_0 = -15.4266$ ($E_0/N = -0.6428$)
* **Best Sampled Energy:** $E_{\text{best}} = -15.4266$ (**100.0% Exact Ground State Match**)
* **Sokal Autocorrelation Time ($\tau_{\text{int}}$):** 14.82 steps
* **Effective Sample Size (ESS):** 438.6 uncorrelated samples / 13,000 recorded iterations
* **Mean Absolute Overlap $\langle |q| \rangle$ (Low $T$):** 0.5215 (cluster condensation in low-lying Parisi valleys)
* **Overlap Variance $\text{Var}(q)$ (Low $T$):** 0.3345 (broad bimodal support indicating multi-valley RSB exploration)

## Experiment 11: Thermodynamic RNA Secondary-Structure Conformational Ensemble

> [!NOTE]
> ### Biophysical Model Scope & Scientific Caveats
> * **Simplified Energy Model:** Nearest-neighbor stacking ($\sigma = -2.0\text{ kcal/mol}$) and base-pairing binding affinities ($\epsilon_{\text{GC}} = -3.0, \epsilon_{\text{AU}} = -2.0, \epsilon_{\text{GU}} = -1.0\text{ kcal/mol}$) are coarse order-of-magnitude approximations. They do not implement the complete Turner 2004 nearest-neighbor parameter set (sequence-dependent 16-parameter stacking tables, loop length entropies, dangling ends, or coaxial stacking).
> * **Curated Small Instance ($N=24, M=24$):** Selected deliberately to permit exact $2^{24} = 16,777,216$ state brute-force ground-state proof. It serves as an ensemble proof-of-concept for thermodynamic sampling, not a drop-in replacement for production ViennaRNA/RNAfold tools.
> * **Pseudoknot Formulation:** The topological cross-pairing penalty ($\lambda_{\text{pseudo}} = +5.0\text{ kcal/mol}$) provides baseline steric crossing suppression; this does not claim general-purpose 3D tertiary structure prediction.

### Overview
Models RNA secondary-structure folding as an energy-based quadratic formulation over candidate base pairs $x_k \in \{0, 1\}$ ($s_k = 2x_k - 1$) sampled according to the Boltzmann distribution $P(x) \propto e^{-\beta H(x)}$. Rather than predicting a single minimum free energy (MFE) structure via classical dynamic programming (Zuker algorithm), this experiment samples the complete thermodynamic conformational ensemble, quantifying structural switching, marginal base-pairing probabilities ($P_{ij} = \langle x_{ij} \rangle$), and conformational Shannon entropy ($S$).

### Hamiltonian & Energy Model ($H(x)$)
$$H(x) = \sum_{k} \epsilon_k x_k - \sum_{(k, l) \text{ adjacent}} \sigma_{kl} x_k x_l + \sum_{(k, l) \text{ conflict}} \lambda_{\text{conflict}} x_k x_l + \sum_{(k, l) \text{ pseudo}} \lambda_{\text{pseudo}} x_k x_l$$
* **Binding Affinities ($\epsilon_k$):** $\text{G-C} = -3.0$, $\text{A-U} = -2.0$, $\text{G-U (wobble)} = -1.0\text{ kcal/mol}$.
* **Stacking Stabilization ($\sigma_{kl}$):** $-2.0\text{ kcal/mol}$ for adjacent paired bases in anti-parallel stems.
* **Steric Clash Penalty ($\lambda_{\text{conflict}}$):** $+15.0\text{ kcal/mol}$ hard penalty enforcing that each nucleotide pairs with at most one partner.
* **Pseudoknot Penalty ($\lambda_{\text{pseudo}}$):** $+5.0\text{ kcal/mol}$ for crossing contacts ($i < i' < j < j'$).
