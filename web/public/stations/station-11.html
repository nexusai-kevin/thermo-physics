The physics

Three ingredients

demo parameters
  • Affinities Each candidate pair carries a base-pair affinity; G–C binds hardest, the G–U wobble barely holds. The browser demo scores them: G–C · 2.2A–U · 1.4G–U · 0.6
  • Stacking Adjacent pairs in a stem stabilise each other (+1.6 in the demo model), which is why folds grow long unbroken stems instead of scattered pairs.
  • Steric exclusion One partner per base, a minimum hairpin loop of 3 unpaired bases, and no crossing pairs — built into the partition function, so forbidden structures carry exactly zero weight.
