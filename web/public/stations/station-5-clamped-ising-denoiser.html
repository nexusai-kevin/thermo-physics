How it works

Markov random field
01 · CLAMP

Observations are pinned

The noisy image enters as observation nodes y, clamped — fixed for the whole run. They are evidence, never updated.

02 · PRIOR

Latent spins prefer company

Behind them sits a latent Ising layer x. Its prior rewards neighbours that agree, which is what “smooth” means here — except at real edges, where the evidence is strong on both sides.

03 · SAMPLE

Gibbs sweeps settle it

Each sweep revisits every latent spin and re-draws it from its conditional distribution. Isolated noise flips get outvoted; structural edges survive because whole neighbourhoods back them.

P(xi = +1 | rest) = σ( 2β · ( J·Σj∼i xj + κ·yi ) )

The probability of a latent spin pointing up, given its four neighbours and its clamped observation. The three sliders on this page are exactly the J, κ and β in this equation.
