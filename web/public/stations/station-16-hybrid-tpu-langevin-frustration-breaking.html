01 — THE INTEGRATOR

Underdamped BAOAB

The variables carry momentum, so the search coasts across flat regions of the energy landscape instead of diffusing. Each step splits into five exact pieces:

BAOAB

Kick, drift, thermostat, drift, kick — the O step is where friction and noise keep the trajectory honest.

02 — THE MEMORY

Dynamic clause memory

Clauses that keep getting violated are remembered and weighted harder, so the solver stops re-breaking the same constraints. Toggle it off in the controls and watch the hybrid stall like the baseline.

03 — THE ESCAPE

Jarzynski–Neal resampling

When the trajectory does lodge in a frustrated pocket, momenta are resampled and trajectories reweighted — a principled kick that restarts the momentum without throwing away the state. Hit Resample mid-run to fire one manually.
