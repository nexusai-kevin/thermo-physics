The physics

V(x) = (x² − 1)²

Minima at x = ±1, barrier of height ΔV = 1 at x = 0. A particle needs a thermal kick of order ΔV to change wells.

dx = −V′(x) dt − J·coupling + √(2/β) dW

Overdamped Langevin dynamics: the noise term shrinks as β grows, so hops freeze out roughly as exp(−βΔV) — the Kramers picture behind the falling hop rate.
