The model

H = − Σ⟨ij⟩ δ(si, sj) · s ∈ {0,1,2,3}
  • Matching pays. Each neighbouring pair in the same state contributes −1 to the energy; mismatches contribute 0.
  • β weighs energy against entropy. A flip that raises energy by ΔE is accepted with probability e−βΔE.
  • q = 4 is special. In 2D the transition sits exactly at βc = ln(1 + √q) = ln(3).
